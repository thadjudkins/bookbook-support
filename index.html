Need help?

Email bookbook.app.support@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add books?

Tap + Book in your library and choose an ebook, an audiobook, or both together from the Files app, including iCloud Drive. Press and hold a book in your library to change its files, details, or cover.

Which file types work?

Ebooks: EPUB, PDF, and plain text. Audiobooks: M4B, MP3, M4A, and other common audio formats.

Why won't a book I bought open?

Book?Book! works with DRM-free files. Books and audiobooks from stores that lock files to their own apps can't be opened by other apps.

How does switching between reading and listening work?

When a book has both an ebook and an audiobook, Book?Book! scans them in the background to match recording time to text. After that, your place carries over whenever you switch. The first scan of a long book can take a little while.

Can it read a book aloud without an audiobook?

Yes. On-device Text to Speech reads any ebook aloud and keeps your place, so you can switch back to reading at any time. The voices are downloaded once and then work entirely on your iPhone, even offline.

What does Book?Book! Pro include?

Reading is always free. Audiobook playback and Text to Speech include a 14-day free trial. After that, Book?Book! Pro unlocks them permanently with a single one-time purchase. There is no subscription.

How do I get Pro back on a new iPhone?

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy
